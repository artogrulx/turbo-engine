Controls

      

        W A S DWalk
        ShiftSprint (or double-tap W)
        SpaceJump · swim up · climb out of water
        CCrouch (won't walk off edges) · dive
        MouseLook around (arrow keys also work)
        Left clickHold to mine a block, click to pick a flower
        Right clickPlace block / flower · use bucket
        Middle clickPick the block you are looking at
        1 – 9 · WheelChoose hotbar slot
        EOpen / close inventory
        EscPause and release the mouse
        TChat (multiplayer)
        TabHold to list the players (multiplayer)
        F3Debug information
      

      
Tip: an empty bucket scoops up still water; pour it on a hillside and watch it run downhill.
